Transparent scoring

Lead scores that show their work

Every lead gets a 0–100 score — and a “Why this score?” breakdown naming each factor and its points: stage, priority, budget band, how recently you spoke, and the source. It ends with the one next action.

An open rulebook, not a model you take on faith. You can read exactly why a lead scored 74 — and disagree with it.

How it works

Questions

Straight answers.

Do my buyers need an app?

No. They open a link and sign in to a portal on your branding. They can install it if they want, but it works in any browser.

Can two builders on the platform see each other's data?

No. Every record is scoped to its workspace, and on the server that isolation is enforced by the database itself through row-level security — not only by application code. A bug in the app cannot leak another builder's leads.

What happens to my data if I leave?

An admin can export the full workspace as JSON at any time — leads, projects, bookings, payments, documents. It's your data.

Can I change the sales stages to match how we work?

Yes. Stages, lead sources and configurations are yours to rename, add or reorder. Three core stages — New, Booked and Lost — are fixed because reporting and the booking flow depend on them.

Is there an API?

There's a read-only API for authentication, leads and workspace metadata — enough to feed a dashboard or a warehouse. Write endpoints aren't published yet. The API reference documents exactly what exists and what doesn't.

Do you take a cut of my sales?

No. A flat monthly price per workspace. Nothing per lead, per user overage, or per booking.
