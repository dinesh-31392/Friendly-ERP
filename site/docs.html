Knowledge base

Setting up

A new Friendly ERP installation starts completely empty. There are no demo accounts and no default password — the first screen asks you to create the platform administrator, with a password you choose.

  1. Open your Friendly ERP address.
  2. Fill in Set up Friendly ERP — your name, email, and a password of at least 8 characters.
  3. Sign in with it on the Platform tab.

Setup runs once. As soon as an account exists the screen is replaced by the normal sign-in form, so it cannot be used again to create a second administrator.

Just evaluating?

The setup screen also offers Load showcase demo. It builds a sample builder workspace with projects, units, leads at every stage, a booking with a payment schedule, and a login for every role. Passwords are generated on the spot and shown once — you can copy them all in one click.

Demo credentials are meant to be shared, so never load the showcase on an installation that holds real customer data. The option only appears while the installation is empty.

Your workspace

A workspace is one builder. Everything — leads, projects, units, bookings, team — belongs to exactly one workspace and is invisible to every other. Your plan, currency and branding are set per workspace.

Every workspace begins on a 14-day trial with no card required. When the trial ends, sign-in is blocked until the workspace is activated; your data is not touched.

Installing the app

Friendly ERP installs from the browser onto a phone, tablet or computer. There is no app store download and no separate mobile build — same address, same account.

DeviceHow
AndroidTap Get the app on the sign-in page, or Chrome's ⋮ menu → Install app.
Windows / macOS / LinuxClick Get the app, or the install icon in the Chrome/Edge address bar.
iPhone / iPadIn Safari: Share → Add to Home Screen. Chrome on iOS cannot install apps — that is an Apple restriction, not ours.

Once installed it opens full-screen from the home screen and keeps working offline. Updates arrive on their own — nobody has to reinstall.

Installing requires HTTPS. If your Friendly ERP is served over plain http:// — an IP address with no certificate — browsers refuse to install it and offline mode is unavailable. The site still works normally; it just cannot be installed until a certificate is in place.

Leads & pipeline

Leads are the core of the workspace. View them as a Kanban board, a list, or a grid, and filter by stage, source, priority or owner.

Stages are yours

Rename, reorder or add pipeline stages in Settings → Pipeline & Fields. No deploy, no support ticket. Three stages are fixed — New, Booked and Lost — because reporting and the booking flow depend on them. You can add anything between.

Working a lead

Open a lead for the full picture: score, stage, notes, activity timeline, and one-tap Call, WhatsApp and Email that open the real app on your phone. Every call ends with a short log so the timeline stays honest.

Site visits

Geo-Verified Site Check-in stamps the rep's location onto the lead's timeline. It turns "I visited the site" into a record.

Bulk work

Import leads from CSV, and select many at once to reassign, change stage or delete.

Lead scoring

Every lead carries a score from 0 to 100, shown as Hot, Warm or Cold. It is not a black box — open a lead and click “Why this score?”.

Five factors, each with its own points:

FactorWhat moves it
Pipeline stageHow deep in the funnel the lead is.
PriorityHot, warm or cold.
BudgetWhich band the budget falls into.
Engagement recencyHow recently you last spoke. This decays.
Lead sourceReferrals, walk-ins and website enquiries convert better.

Underneath, the next best action names the one thing to do — derived from whichever factor is dragging the score down.

Projects & inventory

A project holds towers, floors and units. Each unit has a configuration, area, price and live status — available, reserved, blocked, booked or sold. Book a unit directly from a lead; it is marked immediately, so two reps cannot sell the same flat.

Bookings & payments

This is where a generic CRM stops and Friendly ERP keeps going.

Convert a lead to a booking and the payment schedule generates itself from the plan you pick:

  • 30-70, 20-80, 40-60 — staged splits
  • Construction-linked — tied to build milestones
  • Flexi — your own arrangement

Instalments always add up to the exact unit price — any rounding is absorbed into the final instalment, so the schedule never drifts by a rupee. Mark instalments paid as money arrives; overdue ones surface on their own, and the buyer sees the same schedule in their portal.

Friendly ERP records payments; it does not collect them. There is no card processing or payment gateway. You take money the way you already do and record it here.

Channel partners

Add channel partners with their firm, RERA id and commission rate. When a partner-sourced lead converts, the commission is calculated on the booked unit price and raised for approval.

Attribution follows the partner's identity on the lead, not a name match — so two partners with similar firm names never get each other's commission.

Buyer & partner portal

The portal is a separate front door for people outside your team. It carries your logo, colour and name — not ours — and portal accounts are entirely separate from CRM logins.

Home buyers

Invite a buyer from the lead's Customer Portal Access button. Credentials are generated and copied for you to send. They see their booking, their payment schedule and what is due next.

Channel partners

Invite a partner from their card in Partners. They see the leads they referred and the commission earned on each closed booking.

Both sign in at /portal?builder=your-slug.

Project microsites

Publish any project as a public landing page — no login. Share the link in a campaign; enquiries land straight in your pipeline, routed to whoever has the lightest load, and the buyer gets an instant welcome message.

Roles & permissions

Permissions are enforced in the navigation, on the routes, and in the data — not just by hiding buttons. Typing a URL directly does not get you in.

RoleScope
Sales Executive Only their own leads. Pipeline, inventory, calendar, site visits, calling. No reports, settings, billing or audit log.
Sales Manager The whole team's leads, assignment, reports, sales performance and coaching. Cannot manage users or settings.
Builder Admin The whole workspace: team, projects, inventory, finance, documents, branding, and the audit log.
Branch Team Platform staff scoped to one branch. Onboards builders for approval; cannot approve, suspend or price them.
Super Admin The whole platform. Approvals, plans, module control, credentials, support access.

Managing your team

A Builder Admin adds team members in Settings → Team and assigns each a role. Your plan caps how many can be active — Starter 5, Growth 15, Enterprise unlimited. Deactivate someone and their access stops immediately; their leads stay and can be reassigned.

Branding & subdomain

In Settings → Builder Profile, set your logo and accent colour. They apply across the workspace and every portal your buyers see.

On Growth and Enterprise you also get a custom subdomain for your portal. Subdomains are unique across the platform — if yours is taken you will be asked for another.

Platform & branches

For operators running Friendly ERP for several builders.

Approvals

New workspaces arrive pending and cannot sign in until a super admin approves them in Pending Approvals. That applies to self-serve signups and to builders onboarded by a branch team.

Branches

Branches divide the platform by region or team. A branch team member sees only the builders in their own branch — not a filtered view they can undo, a hard boundary.

Per-workspace control

Open any builder's Manage panel to see real usage — team, records, storage, revenue, last activity — and to:

  • Turn individual modules on or off for that builder
  • Override user, project and storage limits
  • Set a negotiated monthly price that overrides the plan
  • Extend a trial
  • Export their whole workspace as JSON

Support access

Login as opens a builder's workspace as any of their users — no password needed — with a banner and a one-click way back. You get exactly that user's permissions, not super admin ones, so you see precisely what they see. Every use is written to the audit log.

Data & export

It is your data. An admin can export a full workspace as JSON at any time — leads, projects, bookings, payments, documents. Passwords are never included.

Sensitive actions are recorded in the audit log with who, what and when. Deleting a workspace removes its data across every table; it is permanent and asks twice.
